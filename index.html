Service notes

Do you transport passengers only?

Yes. This service is for passenger transportation. It is not a courier or delivery service.

Can I book an airport transfer?

Yes. Airport pickup and drop-off are supported.

Can I book a round trip?

Yes.

Is waiting time included?

Waiting time is charged separately.

How is the fare calculated?

The estimated fare is based on the estimated road distance and selected waiting time.

Are tolls and parking included?

No. Toll and parking fees are not included in the estimated fare unless specifically agreed.

Is the displayed fare final?

No. The website provides an estimate. Final fare is confirmed by the driver.
